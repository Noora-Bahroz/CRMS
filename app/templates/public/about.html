Our story

Driven by service, not surprises

CRMS began as a small branch operation with a simple goal: make renting a car straightforward. Every vehicle in our fleet is serviced on schedule, every rate is published up front, and every branch is staffed by people who know the cars.

Today we serve individuals, families and businesses across our branch network — with the same focus on clear pricing and cars you can depend on.
